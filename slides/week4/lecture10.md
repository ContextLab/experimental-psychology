# Truth and data

<div class="definition-box" data-title="Data and Truth">

- The "universe" produces data
- Math doesn't lie
- Can we conclude that data tells us about truth?

</div>

---

# Analytic flexibility

<div class="warning-box" data-title="Multiple Paths, Multiple Conclusions">

- There are typically many ways to analyze data
- Different analyses lead to different conclusions
- Sometimes those conclusions align... and sometimes they don't

</div>

---

# Matching questions and analyses

<div class="important-box" data-title="Questions vs. Tools">

- Some questions can be answered directly by "obvious" analytic tools
- Other questions can only be answered indirectly, or are impossible even to explore with a given dataset
- Often more complex and abstract questions are harder to answer with simple analyses

</div>

---

# What's in your toolkit?

<div class="tip-box" data-title="Analytic Tools">

- Observation, intuition, and logic
- Simple summaries of data (mean, standard deviation, sorting by value, etc.)
- Traditional statistical tests (t-tests, ANOVAs, correlations, etc.)
- Fancier tests and simulations

</div>
